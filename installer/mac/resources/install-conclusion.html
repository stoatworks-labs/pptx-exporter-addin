Advanced Exporter is in PowerPoint

To open it:

  • Open PowerPoint and any presentation.
  • On the Home tab, choose Add-ins, then Advanced Exporter under Developer Add-ins.

That also gives it an Advanced Exporter button on the Home tab until you quit PowerPoint. Each time you start PowerPoint, open it from Add-ins again.

To remove it, run Uninstall Advanced Exporter for PowerPoint, from the same place you got this installer.
